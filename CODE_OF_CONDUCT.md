# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NoCodeComments is a community project. We want participation to be welcoming, respectful and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Explain disagreements constructively.
- Respect maintainers' time and project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. To report conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile: https://github.com/Aaqibhafeezkhan.
